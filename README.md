# K HO? — Understand What Matters

**K HO?** is an AI-powered document understanding tool designed to help people understand confusing notices, official announcements, forms, and other important documents in simple language.

Many people can read a notice but still struggle to understand what it means, why it matters, or what they need to do next. K HO? aims to solve this problem by turning complex information into clear, practical, and actionable guidance.

Users can paste text or upload a photo or document. The system analyzes the content and organizes the information into five key sections:

- **K HO?** — Explains what the notice or document is about.
- **WHY CARE?** — Explains why the information matters to the user.
- **NOW WHAT?** — Identifies the actions the user should take.
- **IMPORTANT** — Highlights important dates, deadlines, requirements, fees, and warnings.
- **DESCRIPTION** — Provides a more detailed explanation of the document.

The project uses a web-based interface, a Python Flask backend, Google Gemini AI for document analysis, and OCR technology to extract text from images. It is designed with students as the primary target users, while also aiming to help first-time form users and the general public.

Our goal is to make important information easier to understand and help people move from simply reading a notice to knowing exactly what it means and what to do next.

**K HO? — Don't just read it. Understand it.**
